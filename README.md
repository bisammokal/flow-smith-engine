# Automate Now Blueprint

Website Structure Blueprint — “AI Automation Agency”
🧭 1. HEADER (Top Navigation Bar)

Header woh part hota hai jo har page ke top pe fixed hota hai.
Simple aur premium feel dena hai.

Elements:

Logo / Agency Name: e.g. “Preesoft Automations”

Menu Links:

Home

Solutions / Workflows

Case Studies

Pricing

About

Contact / Book a Call

CTA Button (highlighted):

“🚀 Book a Free Demo”
(Right corner, standout color like blue or gradient)

Optional: Sticky header (stays visible when scrolling).

🦾 2. HERO SECTION (Above the Fold)

Ye sabse important section hai — client ke pehle 5 seconds me impact karna hai.

🎯 Goal:

Instantly show:

“We automate your entire client journey — from lead to follow-up — using AI.”

Structure:

Element	Example
Headline (Big Bold)	“Automate Your Entire Sales & Client Journey with AI”
Subheadline (1 line)	“We build smart systems that capture leads, send follow-ups, and onboard clients automatically — saving 20+ hours weekly.”
CTA Buttons	“Book Free Demo” (Primary), “See How It Works” (Secondary)
Visual Element	Simple animation or illustration showing connected apps (CRM, Gmail, WhatsApp, Sheets)
Client Logos (optional)	“Trusted by teams in healthcare, real estate & SaaS” (3–4 small logos)
⚙️ 3. ABOUT / VALUE SECTION (Who You Are & Why You Exist)

Chhoti introduction about your agency + promise.

Headline:

“We help businesses scale using automation — not more manpower.”

Paragraph (2–3 lines):

Our mission is to free teams from repetitive tasks. We design intelligent workflows that handle lead capture, outreach, onboarding, and follow-ups automatically — so you focus on growth, not grunt work.

Visual:
Founder photo or team illustration + “70–80% workload reduction achieved.”

🔄 4. THE 5 WORKFLOW SUITE (Your Core Product)

Yahan tum woh 5 systems show karoge jo tum bundle me offer kar rahe ho.

Section Title:

“Our 5 AI-Powered Workflows — The Complete Automation Suite”

Layout:
Grid of 5 cards (each with icon + short description)

Workflow	Core Benefit
1️⃣ Lead Capture & Qualification	Smart lead filtering, CRM entry, instant Slack/email alerts
2️⃣ Cold Outreach Automation	Personalized auto-emails to new leads
3️⃣ Follow-Up & Meeting Scheduler	Auto reminders + meeting booking
4️⃣ Client Onboarding Automation	Client welcome emails + CRM update
5️⃣ AI Reminder Event	Gemini AI follow-up suggestions via WhatsApp & email

CTA Below Grid:

“💡 Get the Complete Suite for $3,800 – $4,200”
[Book a Free Consultation]

🧠 5. TECH STACK / WHAT WE USE

Show your technical capability visually.

Headline:

“Powered by Leading No-Code & AI Tools”

Logos Row:
Zapier | Make (Integromat) | n8n | OpenAI | Dialogflow | ClickUp | Zoho | Shopify | WordPress | Google Sheets

Mini text:

“We integrate 100+ platforms seamlessly to keep your systems connected.”

📈 6. CASE STUDIES / RESULTS SECTION

Clients love real proof.

Headline:

“See How Businesses Are Scaling with Automation”

Cards layout (3 columns):

Industry	Result Summary	Highlight
🏥 Healthcare	Automated appointment booking & patient record tracking	“80% reduction in manual work”
🏢 Real Estate	Lead qualification + CRM follow-ups	“Closed 2× more deals”
🦷 Dental	AI blog + SEO automation system	“Organic leads +30%”

Button:

“View Full Case Studies” → separate page later.

💰 7. PRICING SECTION

Yahan tum apna bundle system clearly show karoge.

Headline:

“Transparent, Value-Driven Pricing”

Layout:
3 Columns for Client Types:

Type	Description	Price Range
🟢 Coaches / Small Agencies	Full 5-workflow suite setup	$2,800 – $3,200
🟡 SaaS / B2B Startups	End-to-end automation	$3,500 – $4,200
🔵 Enterprises	Advanced + AI custom flows	$4,500 – $7,000

Button:

“Get a Custom Quote” → opens contact form

🧩 8. TESTIMONIALS / SOCIAL PROOF

Show 3–4 reviews (even if they’re from early projects).

Layout Example:

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
“Our entire onboarding is now 100% automated. Saved 15 hours/week easily!”
— Sarah, Marketing Agency Owner

📞 9. CALL TO ACTION (Lead Form Section)

This is where client fills info to start the conversation.

Headline:

“Ready to Automate Your Business?”

Form Fields:

Name

Email

Company / Website

What process do you want to automate? (short text)

Button:

“Book My Free Strategy Call”

Automation:
Form should connect to CRM or Google Sheet (auto-email + Slack alert).

👨‍💻 10. ABOUT THE AGENCY / TEAM SECTION

Human touch — people trust faces, not just logos.

Headline:

“Meet the Team Behind Your Automations”

Text Example:

We’re a small, dedicated team of automation engineers, AI experts, and workflow designers. Together, we’ve helped 25+ businesses automate their daily operations using Make, Zapier, and n8n.

Visual:
Photos or illustrations + “Collective 3+ years of experience.”

🧾 11. FOOTER

Footer repeats navigation + legal info.

Includes:

Logo + tagline (“Smart Automation, Real Growth.”)

Navigation links (Home, Solutions, Pricing, Contact)

Email: support@youragency.com

Social links (LinkedIn, Upwork, Fiverr)

Copyright © 2025 Preesoft Automations
